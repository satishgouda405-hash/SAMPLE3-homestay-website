FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I book a stay?

Tap the WhatsApp button or fill the enquiry form with your dates and guests. We confirm availability and share a payment link within a few hours.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 12:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged subject to availability.

Is food included?

Breakfast is included with all rooms. Lunch and dinner are freshly prepared on request — veg and non-veg options available.

How far are we from Dandeli town and the nearest station?

About 14 km from Dandeli town (25 min). Alur/Alnavar railway station is ~30 km and Hubballi airport ~85 km. We can arrange pickups.

Is the property family and child friendly?

Yes. Garden Family Room, kid-safe grounds, extra beds and activity kits are available.

Do you allow pets?

Well-behaved pets are welcome in the Riverside Cottage on prior request. A small cleaning fee applies.

Is there mobile network and Wi-Fi?

Major networks work at the main lawn and most rooms. Wi-Fi is available across the property, though we encourage you to unplug.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 7 days before check-in. See our Policies page for full details.

Still have a question?

Message us on WhatsApp — we reply fast.

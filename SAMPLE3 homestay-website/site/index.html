Welcome to Sahyadri Mist

A slow, soulful escape in the Western Ghats

Sahyadri Mist Retreat is a family-run forest homestay tucked into the hills near Dandeli. Four hand-built stays, a river at the foot of the property, and hosts who greet you with chai and stay for the stories.

Made for couples, families and friends who want real quiet — good food, clean rooms, and nature that doesn't need a filter.

Experiences

Days that feel like stories

Every stay comes with a little adventure. Join one, join all, or do absolutely nothing in a hammock.

01

Sunrise Forest Walk

Follow our naturalist through mist-draped trails as the jungle wakes up — hornbills, giant squirrels and the smell of wet earth.

90 min · Daily
02

River Kayak & Dip

Paddle the slow bend of the river, then cool off at a quiet swimming spot only locals know.

2 hrs · Seasonal
03

Bonfire & Stories

Gather round the fire with tea, grilled corn and tales of the Ghats under a sky full of stars.

Evenings · Daily
04

Birdwatching

Over 150 recorded species. Binoculars and a field guide are on us.

Dawn · Daily
05

Farm & Village Tour

Meet our farm, pick seasonal produce and learn how the village lives and cooks.

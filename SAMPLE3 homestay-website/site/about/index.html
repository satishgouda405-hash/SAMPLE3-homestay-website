Our story

A family, a forest and a simple idea: hospitality should feel like coming home.

Who we are

Rooted in the hills, raised on hospitality

Sahyadri Mist Retreat began as a family farmhouse. Guests kept asking to stay, so we built one cottage, then another — always using local wood, stone and the hands of village craftspeople.

Today we remain small on purpose. You'll meet the people who built the place, eat what we grow and cook, and leave with stories rather than souvenirs.

Philosophy

Three promises

Slow by design

No schedules, no crowds. Rooms are few so the forest stays loud and the world stays quiet.

Local first

Local builders, local produce, local guides — your stay supports the village around us.

Gently on nature

Solar water heating, rainwater harvesting and zero single-use plastic in the rooms.
